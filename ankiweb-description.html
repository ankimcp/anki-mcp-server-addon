Anki MCP Server – Turn Claude & ChatGPT into your personal Anki tutor!
*** powered by ankimcp.ai ***

Talk to your Anki collection. Ask Claude — or ChatGPT, or any other AI assistant that supports MCP — to build cards from your notes, quiz you on what's due, and clean up the decks you've been avoiding. It works on your real collection, live, not on a pasted export.

Things you can ask

  • "Create 5 Spanish flashcards for common greetings in my Spanish deck."
  • "Quiz me on today's due cards, one at a time."
  • "Turn my lecture notes into cloze cards, tagged bio-exam."
  • "Which decks am I falling behind on?"
  • "Add audio to the cards I made yesterday."
  • "My LaTeX note type is broken — fix it."

Setup

  • On your own computer — free, forever. If your AI runs on the same machine as Anki — Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, Cline, Zed — the add-on serves it over a private address only your machine can reach. Nothing is uploaded, there's no account, and there are no limits. Install the add-on, restart Anki, then point your client at it. For Claude Code:

    claude mcp add anki --transport http http://127.0.0.1:3141/

    The setup guide covers every other client.

  • From your phone, or a web AI — through the tunnel. ChatGPT and claude.ai run in a browser and can't see your computer, so the add-on can give your Anki a secure public address instead. Sign in once, paste that address into your AI — that's the whole setup. The free tier covers 100 requests a month; Plus is $5/month uncapped. ChatGPT walkthrough →

  • Hosted Anki — if you don't want or can't run your computer 24/7. A full copy of Anki on our servers, reachable by your AI around the clock and by you in your browser. Part of the Pro plan ($15/month), still experimental. Learn more →

⚠️ Windows — one extra step for this update

Fixed in v0.29.1: on Windows, updating or deleting the add-on from inside Anki used to fail with an "Access is denied" error and leave the add-on unable to load. From v0.29.1 onwards it just works. The update to v0.29.1 is the last one that needs care, because the old version is still the one running while Anki replaces it.

First: open Tools → Add-ons, select AnkiMCP Server, click Toggle Enabled so it shows as disabled, and restart Anki. Never update or delete the add-on while it is still enabled. Then pick one of these:

  • Update it — recommended. Click Check for Updates and install. Anki keeps the add-on disabled afterwards, so select it again, click Toggle Enabled to turn it back on, and restart Anki. Your settings and your tunnel sign-in are kept.
  • Or delete and reinstall. Select the add-on and click Delete, then click Get Add-ons… and paste the code 124672614. Restart Anki. A freshly installed add-on is already enabled, so do not click Toggle Enabled this time. Deleting also erases the add-on's settings and your tunnel sign-in, so you will have to sign in to the tunnel again.

After this, Windows updates behave like everywhere else. macOS and Linux are unaffected and can update as usual.
